# Art Direction: "The Broadcast Desk" (2026-10-06)

## The idea
A Sunday sports-section desk crossed with a live broadcast truck. Editorial authority, stats rendered like instruments. The site is a *publication about the league*, not a dashboard.

## Why this kills the AI look
- **Light paper canvas** (`#F5F1E8`) — every AI-slop fantasy site is dark navy + gold. Paper is the hardest differentiator.
- **One signal color** (court orange `#E8590C`) rationed like an editor rations exclamation points.
- **Typography with lineage**: condensed sports grotesque + serif italic accents + mono data — the actual 2026 sports vernacular (Druk/PP Neue Montreal/Azeret Mono/Instrument Serif family).
- **Print craft**: halftone dots, hairline rules, ticket-stub perforations, agate-type stat tables. Texture you can feel.
- **Voice**: commissioner's-desk editorial tone. Power rankings read like a columnist. This is the Puzzmo/NYT lesson — craft lives in voice + structure.

## Palette
- Paper `#F5F1E8` (canvas), ink `#16150F` (text), signal orange `#E8590C` (the ONE accent)
- Win green `#2F6B4F`, loss red `#B3402A` (muted, print-like — not neon)
- Rules/lines `#D8D2C2`, faint text `#6E6A5E`
- Team colors stay (identity), but muted to sit on paper

## Type
- Display: Barlow Condensed 800 — headlines, scores, HUGE. Sentence case, not all-caps-everything.
- Accent: Instrument Serif italic — pull quotes, power-ranking titles, editorial asides.
- Data: IBM Plex Mono — every stat, score, record. Tabular numerals always.
- Body: Barlow — UI text, readable.

## Texture
- Paper grain (subtle, felt not seen)
- Halftone dots on section headers and hero moments
- Hairline rules (`1px`) instead of card borders
- Ticket-stub perforation dividers between major sections

## Layout principles
- DRAMATIC scale: hero numbers at clamp(4rem, 10vw, 9rem). No gentle progressions.
- Editorial asymmetry: lead story + sidebar, not even grids.
- Sections separated by rules and headlines, not boxes.
- Tables are instruments: mono type, hairlines, no chrome.

## Ban list (the slop checklist)
Anton/Inter pairing, flat gold on dark, purple-blue gradients, all-caps-condensed-everything, generic rounded cards with drop shadows, graffiti clip-art.
