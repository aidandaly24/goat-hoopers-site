# Two editions, refined — direction 16

This is the original author’s iteration of the frozen direction 16. The first submission remains unchanged under `directions/16/`. See `ITERATION.md` for the exact changes, before/after measurements and checks.

The public edition answers “what is happening in this league?” with the current pairing and dated editorial. Desktop preserves the substantial original arena beside the pairing. Phone places both current team identities and their score states before the court. The first actions lead to the five saved pairs or team profiles.

The member edition answers “what matters for my team?” with a compact identity and adjacent My team / View roster actions, then a matchup/value workbench. Standing, roster count, modeled value, priced/unpriced coverage and computation time remain distinct. The form notebook, completed-week evidence and team wire precede the complete shared league reference. It is a separate composition and order, rather than a private card appended to the public front page.

The unchanged GOAT logo, original arena and player portraits carry the league’s identity. Paper + Slate tokens, Inter and Geist Mono remain the visual language. Typography is root-relative; the requested 200% root-font check actually enlarges text. Open columns and aligned figures establish hierarchy. Ambiguous decorative roster-number boxes are removed. Phone faces become concise portrait/player/owner/value rows, with the value itself retaining the price destination. The directory and standings retain independent space for team names and numeric columns.

States remain public/member, known zero versus pending/incomplete scores, dated stale draft editorial, ready/stale/missing AI, modeled valuation and unavailable history/Power/stats. The static fixture warning remains prominent. Saved AI displays the returned choice independently of probabilities and confidence. All periods, source notes, full player references, prior-manager attribution and complete member valuation remain accessible in native disclosures.

At narrow widths, public matchup precedes court; member identity/actions precede matchup/value, notebook and wire. Full rosters are single-column. Search and sorting use labelled native controls. The menu retains Escape/focus restoration, with a focus-only skip link and immediate visible keyboard focus. No animation, modal, external asset/data request or backend import is introduced.

Forbidden patterns remain invented current awards/history/rankings, inferred model choice, null scores converted to zero, text over busy imagery, repeated generic card grids, missing mobile roster names and nested interactive controls. This remains an offline static design preview, not proof of Next.js routing, authentication, live data, game behavior, native browser zoom, assistive technology or physical devices.
