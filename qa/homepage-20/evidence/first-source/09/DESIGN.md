The question is “What needs my attention in the league, and where can I inspect the numbers?” The first action is View roster for a member, or Find a team or player for a public visitor. The featured current pair remains the first league information on the page.

The composition is a working desk: open columns, aligned tabular figures, readable team identities and compact source notes. Inter carries the league names and editorial. Current matchup, modeled valuation, saved model slate, observed form and editorial opinion are separate source categories. The fixture label is prominent. A known score of zero remains 0.0; a null score is Pending. The featured pair comes from the directory, matching the member’s team when present. Every full roster remains in a native disclosure, including the previous-manager note for NeuralNets’ 2025 record.

At tablet widths the two desk columns stack, preserving reading order. At phone widths the member’s top three become compact rows; editorial becomes three short stories; tables keep units and long names wrap. The 320px layout stacks the AI result below its distribution and the faces list into one column. Search and sort keep visible labels. The persistent menu is a native nonmodal disclosure with ordinary links and Escape returning focus to its summary. A focus-only skip link reaches the main content. No motion or scene runtime is used.

Forbidden patterns: dashboard tiles, terminal styling, fabricated history or projections, inferred AI choice, null-to-zero conversion, decorative live indicators, tiny body type, image-backed text, fake controls or generated team imagery.
