# 20 — Quiet League Index

## Surface contract

- **Question:** What is happening in my matchup, and where do I go next to inspect the league?
- **First action:** View your roster from the current matchup. The opponent and all saved AI picks are adjacent working paths. Public visitors get the same featured current pair without personal content.
- **States:** Public/member previews; known zero versus pending score; incomplete scoring; current fixture versus stale draft editorial; ready/stale/missing saved AI; complete pricing with explicit unpriced coverage; unavailable history, Lineup Power and league superlatives.
- **Narrow layout:** One open column, full-width native league menu, one-line week number, matchup before the natural 16:9 court, vertical saved pairs, complete roster disclosures, two-column player faces, and a real standings table. All main actions have at least 44px measured height on phones.
- **Forbidden patterns:** Invented trends or player statistics, inferred AI choice, current award labels on old editorial, hidden essential missing states, repeated raised cards, decorative gauges, busy-image text overlays, rejected figurines and private production scene code.

## Composition

This is a reference page with the graphic authority of a league register. A heavy top edge, strong section rules, aligned monospaced facts and large week typography supply the hierarchy. The paper stays open. Slate identifies actions; the existing arena, approved logo and portraits provide the athletic character.

The current pair leads alongside the original court image. Team names, managers, records and scores appear once in that lead. A compact member identity line follows it. Five saved AI pairs share one ruled row on wide screens, including each actual distribution and the returned choice. The fixture deliberately returns the 35% team, so choice is printed separately instead of inferred from the larger bar.

Member valuation is an open ledger: modeled total and coverage on the left, three named price leaders on the right, then a native disclosure containing all 24 values. No historical line is drawn. The rest of the register keeps the three dated editorial takes, observed-form notebook, completed-week sample, ten league faces, news, transactions, full team directory, standings, archive and playable game destination.

The directory has independent profile links and native roster disclosures. Search covers team, manager and every player name. Player matches open the matching roster. Sorting supports roster number, team name and season points with stable roster-number ties. Standings have their own labeled sort select and preserve original rank while sorting.

## Truth and provenance

The warning under the main menu explicitly identifies the offline fixture and its synthetic current figures. The hero reads the current directory pair, never the dated edition story. The stale October draft is visibly separated and keeps its original context in a disclosure.

The home score is `0.0`; the away score is `Pending`. Neither is called final. AI stale state is printed in every row and suppresses the full probability bar; saved numbers remain labeled as stale. Missing AI has an explicit unavailable state. Provenance retains returned choice ID, every shown probability, confidence as a separate signal, save time, season/week, model, prompt, evidence and unavailable input/baseline snapshots.

Member observed form, synthetic completed-week recorded performers, modeled FAAB prices and dated editorial opinion retain separate headings, periods and basis. The previous manager of NeuralNets is credited whenever its 2025 record appears. All ten directory containers preserve the 228 actual player references with `/player/id` links.
