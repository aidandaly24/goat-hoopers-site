# Three independent homepage worlds

The earlier twenty-study set was rejected again. Its completion work added useful content but linearized different interfaces into familiar rectangular bands. The implementation choices did that; there is no evidence that a design skill mechanically forced the result. This round preserves that research and starts three clean, independently authored worlds rather than polishing or expanding it again.

Production `DESIGN.md`, its selected palette, section sequence, dense rows, modest-image convention and shared primitives remain unchanged. They are deliberately not visual constraints on this isolated experiment. Security/data boundaries, factual truth, readable controls and responsive access still apply. The briefs supply a small different fact palette to each author, with no exhaustive inventory, section quota, common footer, presentation template or shared stylesheet. The user’s dashboard screenshots were neither supplied to the authors nor copied as design/asset sources.

## Whole-page comparison

- **A, Home court:** one original illustrated room from rafters to painted floor. A suspended score, diagonal roster bench, notice papers and season/practice marks make useful league content inhabit the environment. Phone becomes a sideline walk with a different tall illustration and descending portraits. Priority: the member’s team and next action.
- **B, Sports weekly:** dominant owner-supplied arena image and an overlapping opener headline establish an oxblood sports frontpage. Historical final, a large player cutout, rookie and irregular wire margins continue the same field. Phone changes the crop, headline rhythm and image/text relationships. Priority: the dated opening-edition conversation.
- **C, Season threads:** two unequal historical records begin immediately at different positions; continuous narrative curves, asymmetric fragments and changing scale weave toward the draft opener. The ending is the meeting of those narratives rather than an appended utility band. Phone interleaves records, people and dates with unequal widths. Priority: how two franchises arrived here.

Root compared complete grayscale and designed desktop silhouettes, complete phone images and native reading checkpoints. The structures differ before palette is considered. C’s first submission nevertheless shared B’s large headline entrance; root rejected that entry after the blind submissions and the author replaced it with immediate staggered team narratives. First C source/captures are retained in `evidence/c-first-source` and `evidence/c-first-review`, with matching original hashes. This is a qualitative composition judgment, not user approval or a scored proof of taste. No more studies have been started.

Each design contains a useful whole initial homepage without clicking to expose the bulk of its content. Content priorities differ deliberately. A uses a clearly marked future-dated synthetic member fixture; B/C use explicitly unpublished October draft and historical material. Zero, unavailable, draft/upcoming, prior-season results, NBA/college averages and ownership periods retain their meanings. These studies do not claim to show every existing production field; existing production functionality remains untouched and useful real destinations are linked.

## Targeted defects and checks

A’s desktop bench initially covered names/quotes, and its total extended beyond320px. The author moved the rails/legs out of text sightlines, attached seat segments to portrait bottoms and made the value layout wrap. A missing-glyph arrow became a small inline SVG. Root inspected the corrected actual bench pixels.

The first root-font check did not enlarge fixed-pixel typography. Root corrected the method: snapshot each HTML element’s computed font size, then double it, without reducing chosen type or altering captures. This explicitly emulates text-only enlargement; it is not native browser zoom. It revealed headline/number/header/chronology wrapping problems in B/C. The authors corrected intrinsic minimums, wrapping and flow without clipping text, test flags or another common layout. Current checks use this corrected method; earlier root-font-only observations are not enlarged-text evidence.
