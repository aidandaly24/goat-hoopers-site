## Art and interaction contract

The user question remains: what is happening across this league, and which roster or saved source explains the row? Search/sort the whole working sheet, then open a reader at its origin. There is no hero, card wall, sidebar or nested outlined containment.

Bright white carries the data. A black masthead keeps the approved white GOAT identity readable. Local IBM Plex Mono carries records, values, controls and source prose; local Archivo Black is limited to the compact page/sheet identity. Black table heads and real row rules support comparison. Signal red identifies the active sheet, selected field group and focus/open-state feedback. Underlines, text labels and `aria-current`/`aria-expanded` carry state alongside color. No decorative indices, caps tracking, tight kerning, gradient, texture, new artwork or badge system.

The complete ledger architecture remains: League, Matchups, My evidence for members, Dated editions, Moves & tools. Desktop retains meaningful aligned columns. Phone retains the native Sheet chooser, persistent Fields chooser and labelled records with roster/brief actions before selected fields. At large root text, a content-width query changes labelled fields to label-above-value records, improving reading room while retaining every field. Long narratives use a 66-character measure and increased leading.

The existing history model retains category, query, order, field choice, open row, focus and departure scroll. Profile/tool links remain ordinary real-route links. A small focus correction now makes an originating row trigger visible after closing a reader from far down its contents, clearing the sticky phone field chooser with an immediate scroll when needed. No data or destination was added or changed.
