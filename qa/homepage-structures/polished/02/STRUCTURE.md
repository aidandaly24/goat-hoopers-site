# Court positions

The approved six-position architecture remains intact. **User question:** “Where on our court can I understand this week, inspect my team or find someone in the league?” **Primary action:** choose a labelled court position and read its field notes.

Desktop keeps the full flat court beside a readable foldout. Phone shows a complete vertical court and then the inline reader. At enlarged narrow text, the map uses a wider single lane; detailed rows grow vertically rather than clipping names or values. The selected position is marked and traced toward its reader. Back/Escape visibly returns focus to that origin. There is no modal, drag-only control, repeated panel stack or autonomous motion.

| State ID | Position | Reachable inventory |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `matchup` | Centre circle | Own/public featured current pair, week, both identities/managers/records/scores, incomplete status and all five saved AI rows with provenance |
| `roster` | Left wing | Member identity/actions/standing, modeled total and all 24 values, form/watch/recap/news/moves, unavailable history/Power; all ten faces. Public receives league faces/values and tools without personal content |
| `standings` | Right wing | Ten ranked teams/managers, W–L, correctly scaled PF and playoff facts |
| `edition` | Left baseline | Dated draft game, three complete spotlights, happenings/sources and both dated archive routes |
| `league` | Right baseline | Ten full teams/rosters, 228 unique player references with position/NBA/profile links, local search/sort and qualified prior-season records |
| `wire` | Sideline | All three synthetic moves, dated happenings/sources, Stocks/trade/AI/Arcade/Free Throw actions and provenance boundaries |

`window.goatStudy.states` / `activate(id)` retain these six IDs. Default `matchup` keeps the court at the top; nondefault phone captures begin at the inline reader. All disclosures/search/sort/close/reopen remain actual controls. The ready marker remains `02` and follows font readiness.

The court governs the complete navigation and reader relationship. It is not a hero above the former dashboard. Common data imports remain explicit; no producer, price history or extra ranking is fabricated. Synthetic/current/draft/historical/stale/missing/known-zero qualifications remain intact.
