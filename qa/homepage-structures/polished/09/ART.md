## Art contract

The visitor opens evidence at its source and keeps other evidence open for context. Cobalt connectors identify the native disclosure that owns a body; they do not represent geography, dates, distances or invented coordinates. The current pair, saved model choices, member value/form/wire, searchable league register and dated drafts retain separate local ownership. There is no interchangeable reader.

The allocation supplied technical cobalt, pale-blue paper, dark ink, one safety-orange action, Space Mono and DM Sans. Those choices are now local semantic roles:

| Role | Applied use |
| --- | --- |
| Paper `#edf4ff` | One continuous working ground, with no card frames or decorative grid. |
| Ink `#102438` | Reading and identity. |
| Cobalt `#1649c4` | Real navigation, current numbers and local route connections. |
| Muted ink `#475b72` | Source qualifications and secondary evidence. |
| Surface `#f8fbff` | Open model provenance, kept adjacent to its own returned row. |
| Safety orange `#ff8a35` | The single branch-jump disclosure. Its ink border defines the control boundary. |
| DM Sans | Names, prose, controls and source explanations. |
| Space Mono | The actual Week 3 display, scores, modeled values, statistics, saved times and edition IDs. |

Font, paint, spacing, measure, control, focus and motion values are declared as semantic custom properties. The final stylesheet has 70 unique roles, no literal paint or font-family assignments outside their role/font-face declarations, and no inline style attributes. Phone rules reuse the same roles. No negative tracking, italic accent, gradient, opacity layer, coordinate grid, decorative node index or revision label is presented in the product.

Desktop preserves the two independent working arms and the route-local lower branches. The phone route turns through current evidence, the offset team origin, league lookup and the dated fork. The curve and short elbows connect those actual origins. At narrow or enlarged-text measures, containers reduce the offset and give player/value and form rows readable local reflow. Evidence is never moved to a generic mobile reader.

The ten supplied franchise portraits remain useful identity references with adjacent real names/profile links. They are cropped with `object-fit:contain`, use the approved local map, and remain grayscale so picture color does not compete with the technical route semantics. No court, map, texture or photograph was added as ornament.

## Earned motion

Purpose: show the connection from a pointer-selected branch origin to its newly open evidence. The evidence itself appears immediately and is never hidden or moved for an entrance effect. Only the short cobalt connector changes from folded extension to full extension over180ms, using `transform` and `cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1)`. Native disclosure opening retains every independently open context. The CSS transition retargets naturally if the pointer reverses the action.

Keyboard initiation changes an input marker before the native operation, so it has no transition. Programmatic configurations and Back are immediate. Reduced motion sets transitions/animations to none, including pseudo-elements, and retains the same branch bodies, counts, labels and focus. There are no timers, autoplay loops, RAF loops, score tweens, scroll reveals, delayed essential text or motion dependency.

`motion-demo.json` is a real12-action plan for a fresh/default page in member or public mode, at1440 or390. It opens both dated branches locally, folds/reopens one with Enter, opens league lookup, searches Embiid and returns to current evidence. Actions are click/press/fill/wait only; each wait is240ms, below the600ms bound. The plan does not fake a cursor, scene, record or screenshot.
