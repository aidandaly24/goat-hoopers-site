## Earned motion and actual capture path

Only pointer entry from the wall to its score, total or exact editorial claim can animate. The single destination fact moves from its source bounds to its focused position over **180ms**, using `cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1)`. Related facts are readable immediately; nothing fades in or waits for a reveal. Pointer Zoom out can reverse the visible fact relationship. If its source has scrolled out of view, return is still, avoiding a fabricated flight from an unseen origin.

Keyboard entry/return, capture API activation, query/sort, model-list inspection and nested player reading are immediate. Reduced motion uses the same states, focus and scroll with zero animation. A live preference change cancels the running transform; hidden documents cancel it too. New navigation cancels any previous transition. No autoplay, timers, observers, RAF loop, animated score or persistent idle work.
