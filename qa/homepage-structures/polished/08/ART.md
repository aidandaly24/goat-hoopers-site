## Art decisions

Warm amber and cool blue identify the two sides over charcoal grounds. Each color remains with its team through score, record, anchor, probability, roster and scouting views. A neutral shared seam names the evidence rather than pretending it belongs to either team. Team labels, pressed states and real names also carry ownership, so color is not the only signal.

Local Oswald carries team names, large scores and selected display headings. DM Sans carries all reading, controls, provenance, row data and score labels. Display leading allows wrapping; no tracking hack or italic accent. Portraits are the approved real player assets, deliberately cropped to a useful head/upper-body size while names and quote units stay readable. No court ornament or new images.

The league reader changes to one light cool surface and full-width collection/table reading. It shares the fonts and shell while dropping the rival planes. The dated weekly article keeps its archive navigation and exact editorial. Phone retains the side controls/subject selector and shared score context rather than appending both team dashboards.

Paint, fonts, weights, type scale, spacing and target roles are declared locally in `:root`. Selectors use role variables. Literal font family descriptors necessarily appear in the two `@font-face` definitions; those declarations point to verified local OFL files. No production token changes, gradients, tags, decorative indices, tracked capitals or new panel grid. Existing row/table boundaries retain their comparison/reading job.
