## Direction and geometry

The homepage is four unequal running dispatches: citron for your team/current week, white for league action, steel for scouting, and ink for dated culture. Each surface continues through the complete thread. There are no outer cards, radii, card shadows, repeated section rules, decorative numbers, codes or tags. The meaningful hierarchy comes from content, different measures, display type and the continuous channel grounds.

At 1440 × 1000 the streams measure approximately 366 / 456 / 354 / 264px. Reading widths are approximately 326 / 416 / 314 / 224px. Each is a named, keyboard-focusable independent reader. A team value or AI receipt can be expanded without replacing its neighbors. Cross-links keep the relationship to another dispatch explicit, with a return control tied to the departure position.

Phone uses a visible all-channel index: **Team** (public: **Week**), **League**, **Scout**, **Culture**. Full names remain in accessible labels and named reader regions. The selected label is underlined and weighted. Selecting a channel restores its position; all four positions survive desktop → phone → desktop. The index does not concatenate the streams. A compact GOAT/Stocks/More row and expandable Synthetic concept provenance precede it.

The corrected intrinsic layout remains: zero-minimum grid tracks, shrinkable children, wrapping names, bounded images, width-constrained form controls and real reader overflow. There is no page overflow clipping, transform scaling or offscreen masking. Standard screen-reader-only headings/captions are the only clipped text.

## Purposeful feedback path

Representative pointer path: in Team, scroll to the current pairing and activate **Related: Why the saved model picked the opponent**. League action immediately scrolls/focuses the actual first saved receipt and shows its origin/return path. A single **180ms** background wash acknowledges arrival. It moves no text, delays no content and changes no values. Return restores the departure offset and original control.

Keyboard Enter performs the same focus and navigation immediately, without animation. Reduced motion performs the same immediate path. Changing to reduced motion during the wash cancels it; return or channel selection also cancels it. The normal pointer animation and these still equivalents were exercised, rather than inferred from a screenshot. Hook activation is immediate and does not fake a pointer animation. There are no autoplay loops, scroll observers, score/price tweening, authored frame loops or delayed reveals.
