## Art contract

The visitor finds a franchise by name, initial, standing rank or roster player, then opens that franchise in the directory itself. Large hanging team names and authentic leading-player cutouts carry the identity; an ivory dossier physically separates the names above and below it. There is no equal trading-card gallery, sidebar workspace or conventional hero above a dashboard.

The allocated direction was read by filtering only `id="19"` from `art-allocation.json`: **Franchise yearbook**. The navy field is the yearbook's index; ivory is its opened reading surface. Athletic gold is confined to actual standing locators, selected directory controls, focus and a brief origin cue. It is not a repeated badge system. The approved white GOAT logo remains intact.

Local OFL **Bebas Neue** gives franchise identity its natural condensed athletic proportions; letter spacing stays normal. Local variable **DM Sans** carries controls, managers, facts, prices, dossier titles and source reading. Both files match the catalog's SHA-256 values, and the browser reported both font faces loaded. The supplied portraits retain their original pixels and colors, with deliberate `object-fit: contain` crops. No replacement imagery, remote font, texture, gradient, generated portrait or new dependency was added.

All paint, font-family and spacing roles are local to `style.css`. Paint literals occur only in `:root`; font families are declared only in `@font-face` and semantic family roles. Common facts still enter through the explicit `../../data.js` import. The only variable inline styles encode actual probability widths.

Actual standing ranks are retained; visible internal franchise IDs, decorative section codes and repeated initials are absent. Names, manager/current record, roster count, current opponent, player identity/position/value and public profile actions retain their own readable spaces.

This retains A–Z and rank order without two rows of small letter buttons. The four-way locator dock remains Teams / Find player / Notebook / My team; public mode substitutes About league. Find player focuses the real query. The personal bookmark uses the supplied own-team ID. Entry facts and portraits use separate flexible slots: they sit beside each other when space permits and wrap into separate rows at enlarged text. Portraits are never positioned over names, records or values.

The dossier remains a full-width in-flow insertion. Overview / Roster / Form & team wire are actual content states. Notebook tabs retain slate, standings, dated stories, archive and tools. The compact **Synthetic concept** disclosure stays visible in the header; its full provenance expands within the viewport. Full destination access remains in the desktop GOAT menu, notebook tools and contextual links. No footer-only link is the sole route to a required action.

Browser history now records actual internal views, queries, initial and team order, selected team/tab, roster order, edition, disclosure state, focus and departure scroll. Back/Forward restores them; Close/Escape returns through the dossier origin. The URL contains those selectors and can reload directly into the selected roster. The comparison and public Home mark retain the requested fixture mode. This remains a fixture switch, not an auth implementation.
