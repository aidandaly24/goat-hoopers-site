# 04 — Broadcast front page correction

Local synthetic design concept. This corrects only concept 04's completeness. The frozen `polished/04/`, its ART record and all earlier evidence are preserved.

## Surface contract

- **User question:** What is happening across the league, what is on the programme, and which useful action should I take next?
- **Primary action:** Inspect a real fixture pairing on program; move directly to the relevant broadcast desk or existing destination.
- **States:** Member/public fixture; ready/stale/missing saved AI; five games with known scores and null Pending; dated draft editorial and historical final; unavailable Power and recorded team-value history.
- **Narrow layout:** Sticky native source picker plus explicit Go and selected-score strip. All five games expand into normal document flow. Selection closes that list and focuses the matchup heading below the measured controls. Enlarged text moves the source label to its own row. The full homepage remains below every source choice.
- **Forbidden:** Fixed viewport readers, internal scrolling programme panels, a single selected source replacing the home, fabricated live facts, generated-on-render AI, generic bordered-card stacks, private member reporting in the public view, invented Power/history, new providers/assets/dependencies.
