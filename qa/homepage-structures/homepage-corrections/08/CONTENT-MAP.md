Local review artifact; source frozen after the bounded author pass. No publication, integration, production change or approval is implied.

## Surface contract

**User question:** How do these two sides compare, and what else matters around our league this week?

**Primary action:** Read the named current pair, then open a real team/roster, inspect Stocks or read the full AI page. The optional matchup selector and five Compare buttons change only the lead comparison.

**States:** Member/public fixture editions; saved-ready, stale and missing AI; known zero versus unknown score; current incomplete scores versus the separately dated 2025 final; draft/unpublished editorial; missing roster subtotal, recorded value history and Lineup Power.

**Narrow layout:** Both teams remain present. At 390px, paired team columns stay together while the shared seam becomes a full-width row label; slate actions sit beneath their pair. At 320px, the longer roster lenses become consecutive named halves and standings become labeled rows. Container queries respond to enlarged root text and turn the remaining tight comparisons into consecutive halves, retaining every side and all data. Nothing requires a side toggle. Header/menu and disclosures stay in document flow; primary content has no internal scrolling.

**Forbidden patterns:** A selected subject replacing most of the home; hiding the other team on phones; a raw 228-player directory as page filler; viewport locks; fabricated current results or model output; private opponent totals; invented history/Power; externally fetched assets or runtime data.

## Before → after

The owned polished concept had a useful charcoal/amber/blue head-to-head reader, but eight destinations replaced the entire main region. Phone side and subject controls exposed one team's detail at a time. The correction preserves Oswald display type, DM Sans reading, opposed team planes, the neutral evidence seam, two named scores, local portraits and careful source labels. It replaces the subject gate with one continuous homepage. A light reading interval provides contrast without turning the page into a repeated bordered-card stack.

The complete initial content is already open, below the fold in normal page flow. Only metadata/selection explanations disclose. Full rosters remain meaningful direct links; the homepage curates three player references per selected side.
