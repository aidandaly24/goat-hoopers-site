# Study 16: Player filmstrip

A continuous edge-to-edge photographic player filmstrip and readable bench dossier, using real approved transparent portraits as the entry into ten franchises. Selected portrait expands a wide attached textual dossier; keyboard previous/next and direct team index work. Desktop creates a real asymmetric panorama rather than equal cards; phone uses one portrait and dossier with adjacent-team affordances. League-wide information has its own expanded reading mode.
