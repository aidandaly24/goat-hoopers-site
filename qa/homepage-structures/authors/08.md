# Study 08: Head to head

A true split-screen confrontation: own team and opponent each own one half, with a central scoring seam and synchronized comparative reading. Team facts, players and saved choice align across the seam. League-wide views replace the comparison with an explicitly different full-width reader. Phone gives a legible two-sided score then side tabs, preserving context. Not one matchup hero above a normal dashboard.
