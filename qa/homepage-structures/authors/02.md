# Study 02: Court positions

A full-bleed flat 2D basketball court is the information map. Actual matchup, player values, standings and editorial are anchored to distinct court positions, not placed in a dashboard below. Choose a zone to open a readable adjacent field-notes foldout. Phone shows the whole useful court with labelled targets and an inline detail region; no 3D/WebGL or Muse assets.
