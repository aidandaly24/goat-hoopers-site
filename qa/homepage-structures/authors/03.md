# Study 03: Horizontal chapters

A horizontal chapter-based league publication with genuinely side-by-side, full-width destinations and explicit Previous/Next and direct chapter links. Native horizontal viewport movement, not a row of small cards above a long vertical dashboard. Chapters have different internal composition; phone navigates whole chapters with tap/swipe/keyboard equivalents. Allow readable vertical overflow inside each chapter.
