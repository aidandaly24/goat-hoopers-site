# Study 14: Parallel channels

Four unequal vertical running channels, each a continuous unboxed thread (your team, league action, scouting, culture), with independent reading positions on desktop. Cross-links deliberately relate items across channels. Phone selects a channel with a visible all-channel index and remembers position. Avoid small bordered cards or a fixed central focus pane; the simultaneous parallel streams are the architecture.
