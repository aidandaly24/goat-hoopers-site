# Study 12: Orbital league

The ten teams form a readable orbital league diagram around a central current matchup; actual pair links and orbit positions drive navigation. Choose a team to inspect an integrated radial shelf with their players/value/record; league tools live on named orbit axes. Phone uses a smaller legible orbit plus linear selected detail, not tiny labels. No decorative orbit animation, no scatterplot dashboard or generic donut chart.
