# Study 19: Franchise index

An alphabetic and numeric franchise field guide: a large open directory built from hanging team names, staggered approved portrait cutouts and precise locator marks. A–Z/rank index and roster search are primary; activating a franchise inserts a full-width in-flow dossier that pushes the index apart. Personal bookmark jumps to your actual franchise. No equal-card gallery, no sidebar workspace, no conventional hero.
