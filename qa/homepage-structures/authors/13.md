# Study 13: Folding atlas

A folding atlas of full-width, unequal-height chapter spines. Opening a spine unfolds a large integrated spread in-place and collapses the prior spread, with visible contextual neighbours. This is navigation through an accordion book, not ten independent rounded cards. Default chapter is useful; keyboard/Escape/back restore. Phone retains strong spine shapes with a single readable unfolded chapter.

Stage: grayscale working architectural wireframe only. Read ../BRIEF.md (resolved from this folder). Avoid copying any prior layout, other author or generic hero-and-card anatomy. You may choose the geometry and interaction details independently within this architecture.
