# Study 05: Typographic field poster

A single flowing typographic poster where large actual team names, the known zero score, numbers, roster names and editorial claims form the layout itself. Data is composed in text paths, offsets and interleaved bands, not enclosed widgets. On phone use purposeful oversized word breaks and data paragraphs without clipping or microscopic labels. Index links jump to actual text regions.

Stage: grayscale working architectural wireframe only. Read ../BRIEF.md (resolved from this folder). Avoid copying any prior layout, other author or generic hero-and-card anatomy. You may choose the geometry and interaction details independently within this architecture.
