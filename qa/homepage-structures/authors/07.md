# Study 07: Clubhouse theatre

A lightweight CSS/SVG 2.5D clubhouse stage with a foreground bench, angled back wall and named working information stations in spatial layers. Information is readable HTML, not textures. Entering a station expands it in the stage, with direct station list, close/back/focus. Distinct from a flat court map: side-on stage depth and room transitions. No WebGL/3D libs, no Muse scene/GLB files.

Stage: grayscale working architectural wireframe only. Read ../BRIEF.md (resolved from this folder). Avoid copying any prior layout, other author or generic hero-and-card anatomy. You may choose the geometry and interaction details independently within this architecture.
