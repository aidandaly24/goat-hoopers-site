# 05 revision — Semantic text wall

Grayscale structure only. Frozen for root capture and independent re-review; this is not structural acceptance or permission for full art. The rejected first version and its evidence remain untouched.

## Chosen structure and before/after

**User question:** “Which real fact should I enter, and what is its context?”

**Primary action:** Select an actual score, returned percentage, modeled total, name or editorial claim, then zoom into its source context. Zoom out restores the selected origin.

**Before:** The first 05 was one continuous anchored home document. Bigger type and inline disclosures changed presentation but left the same reading/navigation architecture. The independent review rejected that distinction.

**After:** Only the current semantic layer exists in the working canvas. The overview is a spatial arrangement of actual league facts, not a list of topics. The selected fact remains dominant at the next depth: 0.0 becomes the score space; 35% becomes the five-return field and then a giant selected return; $1095.25 becomes the valuation/name field; a team name becomes its complete roster; the Jokić claim becomes that exact claim with its exact stats. Breadcrumbs record the actual path. Zoom out and browser Back restore scroll and the initiating word's keyboard focus.

This is explicitly distinct from:

- A continuous document: inactive families are absent from the canvas DOM; there is no home section sequence or anchor index.
- A query-first result page: the default is populated facts. Search is a secondary control inside the ten-team name field.
- A left-rail document: there is no rail, side reader or persistent topic selector.
- Horizontal chapter paging: there are no adjacent chapters, Previous/Next controls, swipes or chapter offsets. Navigation follows actual fact relationships and can branch between them.
- A spatial cover over a generic reader: the deeper layers use different working geometry—split score, five offset returns, giant probability with source receipt beside it, selectable valuation names, a ten-team spatial collection, roster prose, three dated claim positions and a recorded-final space. They do not feed one shared article body.

## Desktop and phone geometry

**Desktop:** The bounded overview places the known score at left, modeled total above right, returned 35% through the middle, real team names at right, the Jokić claim lower left and recorded final lower right. These are native buttons containing actual values, names and context, rather than surrounding tiles. Selecting one replaces the overview with that fact's full-width working layer. The original fact's typography remains the spatial anchor; related content appears around or beneath it according to the task. No side pane remains.

**Phone:** The wall becomes an irregular six-column text constellation with paired fact positions. It retains a 2D relationship between zero/wire, return/value, name field, claim/final rather than becoming a topic tab strip. Its canvas can scroll vertically to lower facts. Drill-in retains the giant selected fact; the score separates zero and Pending, returns retain offset positions where readable, the ten-team field remains a two-column name arrangement, and claims retain unequal positions. Intrinsic grids reduce columns when root text is enlarged. One contextual Zoom out control replaces the desktop ancestor breadcrumb links; the current fact stays named. GOAT, expandable Synthetic concept provenance, and the small Go destination disclosure are the only global chrome.

The shell is viewport-height. **`#canvas` is the independently scrolling working region.** A full-page browser screenshot alone does not contain its complete interior. Desktop default fits its canvas; phone default and dense name/source layers extend internally. Root owns viewport and interior evidence captures.

## Craft principles that changed this revision

- The early critic's macroarchitecture test changed the task/reveal/navigation model, rather than another font, border or content order.
- Hallmark custom direction/macrostructure: actual text governs every depth. A semantic zoom must retain its selected fact; it cannot serve as an ornamental entry cover.
- Elaya inline-context/depth principle and the supplied Rauno/Emil continuity guidance: each fact keeps its own context, recorded path and explicit return origin. The canvas is replaced at a semantic depth, not appended as another home section.
- `pbakaus/adapt`: the phone keeps a populated 2D fact constellation and contextual Zoom out; it does not inherit a multi-link header, rail, horizontal pager or old long home sequence.
- Addy accessibility and the project navigation reference: native labelled controls, independent profile links, tested keyboard entry/return, 44px phone targets and separate 320px/200% reflow checks.
- Explicit-import guidance: common facts only; no shared renderer/template, live call, installation or motion dependency.

## Motion thesis / still fallback

Stage one is instant and still; no loops, timers, observers or animation frameworks. Spatial continuity is carried by the repeated selected fact, path, restoration and task-specific geometry.

If the independent structure gate permits a later full build, one earned transition may move the selected word from its wall bounds into its focused anchor position over at most 180ms, with related text immediately readable. Return reverses that relationship. Frequent keyboard actions and reduced-motion mode remain instant with the same state/focus/scroll restoration. Interruption finishes at the latest requested layer. This is a future motion thesis, not implemented full art.
