About this study’s data

This is a static team-home study, with no signed-in session or live league connection. Current records, Week 3 matchup scores and player values are synthetic fixtures checked Nov 12, 2026, future-dated relative to the Oct 30 review. Pending means no recorded score; it is not zero.
