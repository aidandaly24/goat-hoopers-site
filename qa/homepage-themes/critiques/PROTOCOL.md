# Independent visual diversity gate

Do not accept twenty agent names as evidence of twenty different designs. Judge actual pixels. Every first direction must have complete desktop and phone pages in both public/member modes before this gate closes. All originals stay visible even when rejected for rework.

Assess each pair across palette/value distribution, typography, full-page composition, imagery/court treatment, shape language, density and atmosphere. “Same page recolored” or “same cream dashboard with a different heading” is a near duplicate and fails. Related sports identity alone is not duplication if multiple structural/art differences are visible.

Reviewers are fresh critics, not authors of these themes. Each first writes independently without reading other critiques. Contact sheets establish an overview, not lower-page quality. Inspect normal-size top/middle/bottom crops and complete pages before claiming a direction works. Record exactly which files and states were seen, pair coverage, near-duplicate/related pairs, concrete reasons and bounded rework. Avoid numerical taste scores and statistical claims.

Use a pairwise matrix with these qualitative outcomes: D = clearly distinct; R = related but meaningfully different; N = near duplicate/rework required; U = not inspected/uncertain. Every upper-triangle pair must be covered by the combined independent review. No U pair silently becomes a pass. Root reconciles disagreements with actual side-by-side pixels. Each direction also needs coherent whole-page theme and readable controls/data; a unique hero above a shared generic body is insufficient.

Near duplicates are reworked in separate `rework/NN/` folders by their original authors, keeping first versions unchanged. Review the revised pairs again. The full diverse first round goes to parent before user delivery. A suggested shortlist may follow, but a large convergence/refinement round requires Aidan's taste signal. Do not silently pick another similar top three.
