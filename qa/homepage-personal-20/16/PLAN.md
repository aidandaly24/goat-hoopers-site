## Product and spatial composition

The home organizes **clubs you keep in view**, with meaningful content before any selection. It does not organize source questions, metric families, personal/league rails, or a ten-manager roll call.

A twelve-column mosaic has a wide **own-club panel** across the upper eight columns and a compact **In view this visit** control area across four. The next row breaks that geometry: a five-column **current opponent's club** and seven-column **adjacent club in league order**. These are finite, substantive panels, not continuous columns. Optional kept clubs append more visible panels; the page uses one document scroll. The fixed own club and opponent remain useful even with no optional selections.

Each club is led by its actual manager and team identity, current record/rank, and current contest. The actions are **Inspect club**, **Keep in view** or **Remove from view**. No club is called a friend, historical rival or followed account. The default additional club has an explicit reason: immediately adjacent in the fixture's league order. Visit-only choices are in memory and never claim a saved subscription.

The wide own-club panel carries the person's current matchup and a modest roster entry point. One supplied completed-week player may identify its available roster sample; it is not a row of three player cards. The primary action is **Inspect your club**, not repair a source gap. Detailed roster quotes, all-player lists and completed-week evidence live in that local inspector. There are no separate evidence/value/standings homepage sections.

## Local tasks and truth

Choose clubs opens a native labelled selector and adds a visible club panel, rather than replacing or hiding the home. Current opponent and own panel stay fixed. Remove/Clear affect only optional visit choices; controls expose state and keep focus. Every public inspector shows supplied identity, current standing, current game and qualified previous season. Opponent rosters are not invented.

One named native dialog handles inspection, with explicit local subviews for the member's own roster and player detail. Back to club/roster, Close, Escape, trigger focus restoration and browser Back/Forward work. Direct-entry Close has a stable same-persona fallback. No stacked modals. All main club panels are normal-flow content; deep inspection does not simulate auth, trades, lineup saves or notifications.

The source explanation identifies current scores, prices, standings, changes and model outputs as synthetic, checked 30 October 2026 08:30 UTC. Missing is never zero, partial quotes never become a total/rank, current scores never become finals, and no unsupplied history, game count, injury, availability or Lineup Power is invented.
