## Two alternatives, one selection

**A — A public sports front page with personal margin notes.** Lead with the league's five games and weave the member's players into the surrounding coverage. This supplies a lot to browse, but the member still has to find themselves, and it remains close to a scoreboard/list homepage. Rejected.

**B — A continuous, locally illustrated open gym. Selected.** Make a welcoming daylight sports place that is also an ordinary readable website. A shallow clubhouse wall holds the member's useful player context; the page opens onto a broad illustrated playing floor containing the actual matchup; the lower edge of the room brings the other league members and useful destinations together. Original CSS/inline-SVG architectural linework connects these areas. It is authored within the page, not a downloaded asset, canvas, 3D scene, figurine family, or moving background. Every area contains useful HTML content; there is no empty illustrated hero to scroll past.

The environment supplies the page's spatial hierarchy, rather than a neutral shell containing cards. It will be recognizably one continuous composition: light roof/upper-wall outline, one shared wall baseline, an open blue-green floor bounded by a few court lines, and a long lower bench line. Text remains level and untransformed. Illustration never obscures text or encodes invented shots, positions, or performance. This is a sports homepage to read and use, not a navigational game or an image with hotspots.
