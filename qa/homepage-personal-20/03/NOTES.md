NeuralNets instead begins with the six unpriced players and why the available subtotal is incomplete. Its missing score, known opponent zero and unavailable model stay separate. The guest starts with five public pairings, then standings and franchise directory, with real account entry on the current GOAT site.

The horizontal masthead gives Home a current location, uses local roster/league anchors, and points to the grouped footer. Members receive evidence, attached matchup context, a complete visible quote ledger, and the league table. Guest receives pairings, league table, public team details and a public orientation/sign-in companion. Footer groups preserve every destination in the shared fixture. All current-site exits have an SVG mark and an explicit accessible exit label. They navigate to the real site; no production result is simulated.

The initial independent plan included a left rail and Barlow emphasis. Root's early product review identified convergence with another author's plan, without providing peer source. Before implementation, I independently replaced those choices with horizontal navigation, an evidence-led notebook and serif reading/table names. No implementation from the earlier plan was built or reused.

## Working typography

Georgia at 17px with 1.6 leading carries explanatory reading; player names use 15–17px Georgia with ordinary mixed-case names. Section titles are 26px, page titles 36px, both regular weight and restrained tracking. This leaves the players and surrounding qualifications prominent without turning the page into a poster. Body text has a practical 57–58ch measure.

Arial/Helvetica/sans-serif provides 12–16px controls and navigation, and 10–11px table/eyebrow labels. IBM Plex Mono provides tabular comparisons, 13–17px numeric table values and 34px matchup scores. Only numeric comparisons use the monospaced face; it does not carry long player names or explanatory paragraphs. A 150% root text setting increases every rem-based role and the document height without reducing type or introducing horizontal overflow at the checked desktop widths.

IBM Plex Mono is the already licensed local asset from the approved catalog. Its original `OFL.txt` is preserved in `qa/homepage-structures/assets/fonts/ibmplexmono/`. No fonts or assets were downloaded. Logos are the exact approved SVG, without edits. The small player portraits use approved fixture paths, with initial badges when no portrait was supplied.
