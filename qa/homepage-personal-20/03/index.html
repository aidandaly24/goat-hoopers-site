About this edition

A synthetic Courtside edition.

All current scores, standings, prices, changes and saved model outputs on this page were constructed for this design demo. They are not reported league events.

Team identities and player references come from the authorized project fixture; they are not a fresh roster verification. No current NBA team, injury or availability status is supplied.

Counted Week 2 evidence refers to the synthetic October 19–25, 2026 sample. Missing evidence does not mean zero points or a DNP. Modeled FAAB is a quote unit, not a cash balance. Incomplete coverage is a subtotal, never a complete team total or league value rank.

The 2025 records and final retain project historical references. NeuralNets’ 2025 record belongs to the previous manager, under QBs Gremlins. The approved GOAT court image is artwork, not photographic evidence of a matchup or historical final.

Source: shared data-only fixture. Snapshot: October 30, 2026 at 08:30 UTC. There is no live API, account session, AI generation, pricing or mutation connection.
