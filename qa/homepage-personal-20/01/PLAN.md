# Concept 01 — A working clubhouse

Reaves returns to a scored matchup and new counted-player evidence; NeuralNets first needs six missing quotes explained and a missing matchup score kept distinct from zero. Guests need the five public pairings, the league table and actual account entry. These are three useful priorities, rather than one headline with substituted names.

A compact persistent GOAT rail holds Home/current location, page landmarks, and the most useful external destinations. The working page has a small context bar, modest team identity, matchup or coverage priority, changes, visible player evidence, complete searchable roster table and public standings. The guest page instead has all five pairings, sign-in context, standings and historical final context. The footer groups every existing destination and explicitly explains exits.

Barlow Condensed gives mixed-case section and team headings a recognisable sports character without taking over reading. Arial/Helvetica at 15–16px handles reading and controls; IBM Plex Mono handles scores, prices and numeric columns. Names wrap naturally. Normal desktop content density is deliberate; controls do not shrink at 1120px or when text grows.

Primary actions inspect player, team and matchup evidence in a native dialog; Escape, Close, focus return and backdrop dismissal are supported. Roster search is local. Page landmarks preserve normal page flow. No account or mutation is simulated. The obvious rejected default was a full-width arena hero and editorial tagline followed by interchangeable cards.
