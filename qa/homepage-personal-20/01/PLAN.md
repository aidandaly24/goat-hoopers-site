# Concept 01 — The dark GOAT clubhouse

This is the revised implementation of the same author’s concept after the first product gate. The neutral rail/table composition was replaced with an image-led dark sports website; the preserved baseline remains in repository history.

The first three useful actions are:

1. Open this week’s matchup, with current synthetic scores and saved context when supplied.
2. Get closer to the team’s players, then open the complete roster for quotes and counted-week evidence.
3. Explore the competition: other pairings, nearby league positions, public team profiles and the full field.

The page has horizontal GOAT navigation, a purposeful original-arena matchup feature, a visible team/season pulse, portrait-led roster discovery and completed-week points, the other four Week 3 pairings, a three-team league-race slice, the actual game preview and league destinations, then a complete semantic footer. Full roster and full standings are local inspection views; the homepage already contains substantial useful content without opening them.

Reaves leads with the 184.50–171.00 scored matchup, its saved 56/44 estimate and the Embiid/Edwards/Şengün roster group, alongside five supplied Week 2 players. NeuralNets leads with its Papichooter matchup, an honest Pending versus recorded 0.00, Giannis/LaMelo/AJ roster portraits, three counted players and a different league-race slice. Missing quotes are quiet supporting context, not a repair task. Guest sees public pairings, four public roster portraits, the early leaders and explicit actual-site sign-in, without claiming a private team.

Oswald supplies the strong sports/team/section hierarchy; Arial/Helvetica supplies mixed-case reading and controls; IBM Plex Mono supplies scores and numeric data. Approved original arena, portraits, exact white GOAT logo and existing game preview provide the visual identity. Dark charcoal and warm light text use a restrained court-green accent for selected data and primary actions.

Native local matchup, player, roster, table and team views have Back, Close, Escape, preserved search, scroll and focus. No login, roster edit, trade submission or generation is simulated. The revised design rejects the utility dashboard and a giant promotional poster: the arena is the setting for the manager’s actual next task.
