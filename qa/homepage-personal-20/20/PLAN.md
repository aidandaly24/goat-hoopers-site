## Two alternatives and selection

- **Personal Match Centre:** a broad fixture masthead, opponent context and a named-player scouting area. Rejected at this checkpoint: these subjects alone would reproduce familiar sports report geometry, especially with a large score and rectangular sections.
- **Permanently open personal action index — selected:** the homepage is the member's working entrance into GOAT. A right-hand route rail supports an unboxed, branching index on the left. Each action branch already contains substantial named facts in normal flow. Inspection expands the member's work; it does not supply all of the page's substance after a click.

The returning-member need is to start with one's own team and people and reach the appropriate GOAT task without first translating the site's tool names. This is a useful reading page as well as a route into the product. It is not a chronology, a full roster, a standings dashboard or a narrative comparing two teams.

## Whole-page geometry and visual world

Use a fluid desktop frame with about 48px outer gutters at 1440, 32px at 1120. The main content occupies approximately 75–79% of the frame; a 208–240px right rail occupies the rest, separated by a generous open gutter. The rail stays in the document and may be sticky when it fits; it must never become a clipped internal reader.

The main column begins with the exact GOAT logo, compact synthetic label, then the member's actual team name in broad mixed-case type. Manager, current record/rank and a short current-matchup line are visible in the first fold. There is no large promotional hero or decorative score.

Each branch has a hanging action heading to its left and substantial open content to the right. Branches have unequal widths and staggered indents: the player review opens at the main left edge; squad inspection is inset about 10%; the matchup is shorter and returns to the left edge; the public league branch uses the wider lower area. This is one open action index, not equal boxes or full-width rectangular bands. No decorative numbering, nodes, connector diagram or oversized arrows.

The world is bright and graphic: white reading canvas, black broad typography, a citron navigation surface, and a restrained warm action accent. The rail uses compact groups, not enormous menu text. Most visual energy comes from real names and useful data. No athlete wall, editorial portrait treatment or invented avatars. The approved free-throw preview appears once in the lower tool context, at a modest landscape size. The arena is optional and will be omitted if it merely repeats decoration.

## Typography roles

- Reading: DejaVu Sans, Trebuchet MS, sans-serif, 17px / 26px, normal mixed case, about 55ch maximum explanation measure. The installed DejaVu face gives explanatory passages wide, clear counters and a different texture from the local headline face.
- Controls/navigation: DejaVu Sans, Verdana, sans-serif, 13–14px / 20px at bold for small rail entries and 15px / 22px for main actions. Native labels and focus remain explicit.
- Names/headings: authorized local Space Grotesk variable, 500–650; main team name about 48–56px / 1.05, hanging branch headings about 28px / 32px, player names 18–22px / 26px. No condensed display face, all-caps word wall or synthetic weight.
- Data: the same Space Grotesk with lining tabular figures; 20–28px values alongside their units, not monospace. Dates are 14px / 21px reading text. The local font's `tnum`/`lnum` support was verified during discovery; rendered alignment still requires browser checks.

Long names wrap. Enlarged text grows the layout, including the right rail. No hard text heights or shrinking names. Preserve the existing local font's OFL notice; no downloads or dependencies.
