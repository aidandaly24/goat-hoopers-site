About this synthetic sample & its sources

This is an independent design prototype. Current scores, standings, prices, player points, sample updates and model estimates were constructed for this demo, as of 30 October 2026, 08:30 UTC. They are not reported league events. Identities, player references and approved assets come from the authorized GOAT project. NBA team labels are intentionally omitted.

2025 history is retained project reference material. NeuralNets' 2025 history belongs to the previous manager, as QBs Gremlins. Prices are modeled FAAB, not cash balances. No recorded price-history series or game-by-game evidence is supplied. The saved model is an experimental sample, not calibrated odds.

No live league, account, market, AI, database or storage connection. Local inspection does not establish production behavior. Links marked “on GOAT ↗” leave this prototype for the existing site. Demo persona selection is synthetic test state, never real account authorization.
