## What is deliberately not accepted yet

The three finished sources establish materially better website completeness and personalization than the rejected poster batch. They are still conservative neutral, data-heavy directions. Their display and reading typography varies, but all three use IBM Plex Mono for data and two use sans working bodies. This is insufficient evidence for the final twenty's requested visual range.

Several early discovery plans for04–14 independently repeated open roster/standings tables and a Georgia/Lora + system + IBM type pattern. Those plans are not accepted builds. Root has flagged the convergence, asked later authors for three private architecture alternatives and explicitly made a full roster on the homepage optional. 15–20 now propose some proportional data, different reading faces and page priorities; actual pixels still need review. The same authors will revise weak plans after the parent's early review, with no common replacement shell or metaphor recipe. No new full build has started for04–20.

Parent review should assess repeat-visit usefulness and the complete first fold, two-team/guest behavior, useful navigation/footer, amount of text/status repetition, density and visual character.
