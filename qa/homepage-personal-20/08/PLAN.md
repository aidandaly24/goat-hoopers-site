# Concept 08 revised discovery — A place to understand your players

Discovery plan only. Active plan for author 08; replaces the table-first baseline. No presentation source has been built. The early-three coordinator gate remains pending.

## Product decision

Make the manager's own players the sustained subject of a complete league website. A returning visitor should be able to read a specific player's supplied evidence, find a different owned player, and inspect their opponent without a large score presentation or an exhaustive roster on home. Missing evidence and missing quotes remain calm qualifications beside the relevant content. They do not become the visitor's job to repair.

The chosen architecture is an asymmetric reading page with a persistent navigation rail, one continuous player column, and an open league-context column. A single approved player portrait anchors the reading column. Evidence notes and selective roster groups continue beneath it. League context is available alongside that reading rhythm, rather than becoming a separate competition show after three athlete cards.

## Selected silhouette

At 1440 CSS px, the page has a roughly 176px navigation rail, 680–720px reading column and 280–300px league column, separated by generous open gutters. This is a website canvas with a readable center of gravity, not a panel grid. The rail's logo and links may stay in view while space allows; at enlarged text it returns to normal flow rather than trapping or clipping its contents. Neither content column is sticky or independently scrollable.

The rail uses the exact approved white GOAT horizontal logo on ink blue, then Home (current), Your players for members / Teams for guest, League, Stocks and Arcade. Local sections use anchors. Stocks and Arcade clearly identify their current-GOAT-site destination. A quiet account entry sits at the rail's foot: My Team for member demos and Sign in for guest, both labelled exits. The full destination inventory is discoverable in the footer; there is no enormous all-route header.

A short utility line across the content area contains Home / team name or Home / League, the compact Synthetic demo label, the fixed October 30, 08:30 UTC fixture timestamp and the native Demo persona select. The selector is explicitly demonstration state, separate from membership. It defaults to Reaves, supports reaves/neural/guest, preserves stage and sets html.dataset.persona. ## Local behavior and truthful limits

Read player record opens an in-flow named region below that study or list item. It displays only the supplied identity, listed position, completed-week total/status, exact period and independent current quote/timestamp. It says that detailed game receipts, game counts, minutes and stat components are not supplied. No reconstructed game log, injury story or performance formula is invented. Focus goes to the region heading; Close or Escape restores the trigger. The region has normal height, no focus trap and no internal scroll.

Find a roster player filters the member's own fixture records, announces a concise result count and retains the query when nothing matches. Clear search restores the initial state. Explore listed guards/forwards/centers opens a normal-flow group list with player inspection; the list is a deeper task, while the substantial homepage content stays visible. No roster edits or saves exist.

Inspect team/pairing reveals supplied public identity, record, opponent, known/missing scores and, where available, the separately labelled saved model state. A clear Back to player reading or Close restores the trigger. Any hash/history state introduced during build must support Back/Forward without changing persona or unexpectedly resetting page context. Source disclosures use native details; ordinary navigation uses links; state changes use buttons.

At 1120 CSS px the rail narrows and the league column flows after the opening study and evidence if both content columns no longer fit comfortably. All league content remains visible through normal scroll. At enlarged text, navigation wraps or returns to flow and columns stack. No ellipsis, fixed content height or scaled-down reading text is used. This remains desktop planning; no phone captures or phone-specific design are planned.

The later build must provide semantic landmarks, keyboard skip access, visible focus and 44px principal control targets. Still/reduced-motion users receive the same content and tasks. No animation is necessary. It must implement the required structure stage and ready marker only after fonts/content settle. None of these rendered checks has been performed during discovery.

The compact Synthetic demo label and source explanation remain visible and accessible. Current scores, prices, standings, observations and saved outputs are constructed. Identities are authorized fixture references, not fresh production verification. Historical periods and previous-manager qualifications remain explicit. There are no live API/auth/AI/market calls, submitted trades, lineup mutations, notifications, invented history or Lineup Power snapshot.
