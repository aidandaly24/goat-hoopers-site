# Selected A · Swept horns

Aidan selected **A · Swept horns** on October 9, 2026 (“A swept horns btw”). This is the current editable source family for publication. It replaces the circular horned basketball in the existing production logo URLs and `src/app/icon.svg`.

The long muzzle, broad ears, outward swept horns and beard are the exact selected A vector geometry. Larger emblems retain a quiet basketball seam suggestion; micro variants omit interior detail to preserve the silhouette below 48px. Black, clay, white and cream variants retain the existing colors. Horizontal SVGs are 1000×365, primary lockups 900×900, emblem/micro SVGs 640×640. Existing consumers and filenames stay intact.

All original GOAT, HOOPERS and DYNASTY BASKETBALL outlines, transforms and spacing are retained byte for byte after the emblem group. No font installation is required. SVG masks make the cuts transparent on light and dark backgrounds. The original Selected Hybrid package remains a historical source in the adjacent folder; its archive hashes and rights/provenance are unchanged. New project-authored goat paths do not assign a blanket license to retained lettering or upstream artwork.

`PROVENANCE.json` pins all 15 editable vector variants, the adaptive favicon source and six runtime copies. The favicon retains clay on light tabs and uses the existing cream on dark tabs through an SVG-local `prefers-color-scheme` rule; its silhouette is unchanged. `scripts/verify-swept-horns-logo.py` validates dimensions, transparent/correct palette variants, identical wordmarks, runtime/source byte identity, selected A anatomy and preservation of original source and other-owner files. The light/dark/header/16/32 proofs are under `qa/selected-swept-horns/`.
