GOAT HOOPERS | Selected hybrid | Round 02

YOUR CHOSEN COMBINATION
The horn-and-basketball emblem from the middle concept, paired with the
Clubhouse lettering and warm colors from the right-hand concept.

PRIMARY COLORS
Burnt clay  #A54429
Warm tan    #F0D5B5
Dark brown  #392A23

LOCKUPS
Primary: centered emblem above the heritage GOAT HOOPERS name and the
DYNASTY BASKETBALL descriptor. Transparent 900 x 900 PNG and editable SVG.
Horizontal: emblem beside the two-line name, without the small descriptor.
Transparent 1000 x 365 PNG and editable SVG.

EMBLEMS
Emblem: the selected horn, basketball, and beard silhouette, retaining the
full ball-seam detail. Transparent 640 x 640 PNG and editable SVG.
Micro: the same silhouette with two seams and heavier strokes. Use this
variant below 48 px or wherever the ball detail becomes difficult to read.
Small transparent exports are included at 16, 32, and 64 px in clay and white.

COLOR VARIANTS
Primary, emblem, and micro: clay, black, white, and warm-tan/cream.
Horizontal: clay, black, and white.
White and cream assets are intended for dark backgrounds.
All artwork has a transparent background; the review board shows background
colors as an application preview.

PRODUCTION NOTES
All lettering is outlined, so viewing the SVGs does not require a font install.
The symbol and typography remain editable vector shapes. Original SVG strokes
remain editable as strokes where appropriate.
The primary lockup uses optically centered name lines and a shared center axis.
Keep the symbol by itself for tiny spaces rather than shrinking the whole name.
For embroidery, use a one-color mark and obtain a digitized stitch proof before
production. The included flat previews are not physical embroidery proofs.

STATUS
This is a refined flat design for review. No website changes or Blender renders
were made. The earlier three-option exploration is preserved separately.
